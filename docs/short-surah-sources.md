## Pronunciation and makhraj

The current release does not invent Bengali phonetic spellings. Bengali script cannot represent every Arabic articulation contrast reliably. The screen therefore shows a limited general makhraj reminder and explicitly advises learning recitation from a qualified teacher. Add verse-by-verse Bengali pronunciation only after review by a qualified Quran/tajwid teacher, and keep it separate from the Quran text and translation.
