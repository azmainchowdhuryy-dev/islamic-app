# Noor Hadith and Dua content sources

## Verification policy

A record is included only when its source collection and individual reference can be identified. Qur'anic supplications are tied to their verse references. Hadith-based supplications and narrations in this revision are restricted to Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim and retain direct Sunnah.com references. The app's `verified` flag means the source text and reference were checked against the cited source. It does not certify the project's Bengali or English paraphrase as a scholarly translation.

## Hadith collection

- The existing 100 additional narrations are selected from Sahih al-Bukhari, with individual hadith numbers and direct Sunnah.com references.
- Ten further narrations were added from Sahih al-Bukhari: 349, 351, 354, 359, 361, 365, 380, 400, 500 and 520.
- Arabic text for the ten additions was cross-checked against [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) and each number was checked against its direct Sunnah.com page.
- Bengali and English descriptions are Noor-authored concise meaning summaries. They are not copied verbatim from a third-party translation and still need qualified language and scholarly review before being advertised as scholar-approved translations.

## Dua collection

- The collection was reduced from 181 to 99 entries after a stricter source review.
- It now contains 81 Qur'anic supplications and 18 supplications with references in Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim.
- Entries imported from the community DeenOne dataset were removed unless their Qur'anic source or a direct Sahih collection reference could be retained and checked. Records with unclear, mixed, or mismatched references were excluded.
- The remaining hadith-based entries keep direct Sunnah.com links. The Qur'anic entries keep their Quran.com verse references.
- Morning and evening remembrances remain in their own folder: 9 entries. The remaining 90 entries are grouped under their relevant daily-use topics.
- Bengali phonetic spellings are not displayed as verified pronunciation. Qur'anic Bengali pronunciation must be prepared and checked by a qualified Qur'an and Tajwid teacher.

## Totals and limits

The current branch contains 198 Hadith and 99 Duas. The added narrations are from Sahih al-Bukhari, and the retained hadith-based Duas are sourced from Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim. These source restrictions provide a strong basis for the authenticity of the narrations, but an absolute 100% guarantee of every translation or interpretation cannot honestly be made without review by qualified scholars.
