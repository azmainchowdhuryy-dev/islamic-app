# Noor Hadith and Dua content sources

## Verification policy

A record is included only when its source collection and individual reference can be identified. Qur'anic supplications are tied to a Quran.com verse reference. Hadith-based supplications retain direct Sunnah.com references. Each narration is kept only where its collection reference and reported grading can be checked. Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim reports are Sahih; Sunan Abi Dawud and Sunan Ibn Majah additions are included only where a cited grading is Sahih or Hasan. The app's `verified` flag means the source text and reference were checked against the cited source. It does not certify the project's Bengali or English paraphrase as a scholarly translation.

## Hadith collection

- The collection contains 198 Hadith.
- The 100 additional narrations in the earlier expansion are selected from Sahih al-Bukhari, with individual hadith numbers and direct Sunnah.com references.
- Ten further narrations were added from Sahih al-Bukhari: 349, 351, 354, 359, 361, 365, 380, 400, 500 and 520.
- Arabic text for the ten additions was cross-checked against [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) and each number was checked against its direct Sunnah.com page.
- Bengali and English descriptions are Noor-authored concise meaning summaries. They are not copied verbatim from a third-party translation and still need qualified language and scholarly review before being advertised as scholar-approved translations.

## Dua collection

- The collection was reduced from 181 to 87 entries after a stricter source review, then expanded to 99 after adding a separately sourced morning and evening remembrance set.
- It now contains 73 Qur'anic supplications with direct Quran.com verse references and 26 hadith-based supplications with direct Sunnah.com references.
- Entries from community datasets were removed when their Qur'anic reference could not be tied to a direct Quran.com verse page, or when the Hadith reference was unclear, mixed or mismatched.
- The retained hadith-based entries keep direct Sunnah.com links. The Qur'anic entries keep their Quran.com verse references. The new morning/evening set includes Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud and Sunan Ibn Majah references, with Sahih or Hasan grading noted where applicable.
- Morning and evening remembrances are kept in their own folder: 15 entries. This set includes three short Qur'anic surahs whose morning/evening recitation is supported by Sunan Abi Dawud 5082, alongside authenticated supplications from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud and Sunan Ibn Majah. The remaining 84 entries are grouped under their relevant daily-use topics.
- Bengali pronunciation guides are shown for all 15 morning/evening entries. They are approximate reading aids, not a substitute for learning makharij and tajwid from a qualified teacher.
- Bengali phonetic spellings are shown as approximate reading aids. They are not certified tajwid transcriptions and do not replace learning makharij from a qualified Qur'an teacher.

## Totals and limits

The current branch contains 198 Hadith and 99 Duas, including 15 morning/evening entries. The ten newest Hadith records are from Sahih al-Bukhari. The morning/evening supplications use source-specific Sahih or Hasan narrations, with the grade recorded where applicable. These source restrictions provide a strong basis for the authenticity of the narrations, but an absolute 100% guarantee of every translation or interpretation cannot honestly be made without review by qualified scholars.
